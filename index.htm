Curtis Dyreson's Home Page

I am a Professor in the School of Computing at Utah State University . I research and teach in the areas of database systems and programming languages. My interests include temporal databases and hierarchical databases. Prior to Utah State University, I was on the faculty at James Cook University , Bond University , and Washington State University , and I was a visiting assistant professor at Aalborg University , I am a graduate of New College and obtained my Ph.D. from the University of Arizona under the direction of Richard T. Snodgrass . I was happy to be awarded the 2015 SIGMOD Contributions Award
